Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony begins at 2:30 PM. We kindly ask that you arrive early so you may be comfortably seated. The celebration continues immediately afterward at The Manila Hotel.

Will there be parking?

Parking at San Sebastian Church is limited, so we kindly ask that you arrive early and, where possible, travel together. At The Manila Hotel, parking is primarily by valet, as self-parking is very limited. If you are bringing your own car, please expect to use the valet service upon arrival.

Is there a dress code?

Yes, our dress code is Formal Attire. For further details, please see here.

Can I bring a plus one?

If your partner is invited, their name will appear right beside yours when you search on our RSVP page. Due to limited space, we’re unable to accommodate guests who aren’t listed. Thank you for understanding.

Are children welcome at the wedding?

Our guest list is limited to adults, with the exception of children in our immediate family. We hope you can enjoy a night out and celebrate with us.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

We want all our guests to enjoy the celebration. If you have any allergies or dietary restrictions, please let us know in your RSVP, and we’ll do our best to accommodate them.

Do you have a gift registry?

Your presence is all we need. Should you wish to give a gift, a monetary contribution toward our future together would be warmly appreciated.
