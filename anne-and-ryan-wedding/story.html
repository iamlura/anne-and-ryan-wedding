Our Story

Ryan and Anne met over eight years ago in senior high school, where he sat behind her in English class. He realized he had a crush on her when she recited with such eloquence that their professor remarked, “Spoken like Shakespeare himself!” It was then that he decided to work up the courage to approach her.

They have been best friends ever since, sharing a journey filled with love and laughter. A few years after graduation, they felt it was the right time to start planning the proposal together. Six months later, in September 2025, they returned to their favorite place in the world, Hokkaido, Japan, where he asked for her hand in marriage. Of course, she said yes! Together with their two furry children, Dandi and Pami, they are excited to begin this next chapter of their lives.
